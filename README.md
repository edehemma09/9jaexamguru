# 9jaExamGuru — Learn&Pass

> Nigeria's free exam preparation platform for JAMB, WAEC, NECO & GCE.

## 🎓 About

**Learn&Pass** is a free, mobile-first educational platform built for Nigerian secondary school and university-aspirant students. It provides:

- ✅ 50,000+ verified past questions (JAMB, WAEC, NECO, GCE, NABTEB)
- ✅ Timed CBT simulation — mirrors the real JAMB interface
- ✅ Daily education news (JAMB updates, WAEC timetables, scholarships)
- ✅ Progress tracking per subject and exam type
- ✅ 100% free — no subscriptions, no hidden fees
