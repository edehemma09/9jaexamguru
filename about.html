About Learn&Pass

Built by Nigerians, for Nigerians. A free educational platform dedicated to helping every student pass.

Our Story

Born from a
Simple Belief

Every Nigerian student deserves access to quality exam preparation — regardless of where they live, which school they attend, or how much their family earns.

We are young Nigerians — former students, secondary school teachers, and software developers — who saw that the best exam resources were either locked behind expensive subscriptions or scattered across unreliable websites.

"We built Learn&Pass because we believe a student in Maiduguri deserves the same exam preparation tools as one in Victoria Island."

So we built one clean, fast, mobile-first platform — with verified past questions, a real CBT simulator, and daily education news. All free. All for Nigeria.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision
& Community

Our Mission

Reliable, free and innovative exam preparation resources that empower every Nigerian student to achieve academic excellence — regardless of background.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no student fails a public exam simply because they lacked access to quality study materials. Equal tools for every student.

Our Community

12,000+ students, teachers and parents who trust Learn&Pass daily to prepare for Nigeria's biggest exams. Growing every week.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2020

Platform Founded

Started as a lean JAMB question bank by three Nigerian developers with a shared dream of free education.

2021

WAEC & NECO Added

Expanded the library to cover WAEC and NECO following overwhelming demand from SS3 students.

2022

CBT Module Launched

Built a full timed CBT simulation, modeled on the real JAMB exam interface and validated by students.

2023

10,000 Students

Crossed the milestone. Daily education news section launched to keep students updated on exam changes.

2026

50K+ Questions

Library now holds 50,000+ verified questions across six exam types including international certifications.
